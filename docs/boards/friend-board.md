# Layout
Column numbers 1–7 are printed above the grid and row numbers 1–5 on its left. He gives the column first, then the
row ([Interaction model](../interaction-model.md), mode B).

| row \ column | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | A | B | C | D | E | F | G |
| **2** | H | I | J | K | L | M | N |
| **3** | O | P | R | S | T | U | W |
| **4** | Z | Ż | Ź | X | Y | Ń | *(empty)* |
| **5** | Ć | Ę | Ą | Ś | SZ | CZ | *(empty)* |

Examples: K = column 4, row 2; W = column 7, row 3; SZ = column 5, row 5.

# Decisions (author, 2026-11-11)
- **Blinker copies the board 1:1.** He knows it by heart, so the letters, their positions and the two empty cells
  stay as they are on paper. Any change is made later, together with him.
- The board has **no Ł, Ó, Q or V**. We keep it that way for now; adding letters is a question for the trial (stage 6).
- **SZ** and **CZ** are single cells, so a cell value can be two characters long
  ([Board format](../board-format.md)).
