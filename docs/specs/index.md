# Specs

One folder per feature: `NNN-slug/spec.md` (`type: Spec`) and `NNN-slug/tasks.md` (`type: Task List`), created with `/plan`.

# Specs
* [001: Application skeleton](001-app-skeleton/spec.md) - Stage 1. Angular PWA shell, i18n, a11y, Vitest, Playwright + axe. [Tasks](001-app-skeleton/tasks.md).
* [002: CI and local verification](002-ci-verify/spec.md) - Stage 1. `npm run verify`, pre-push hook, full CI, Lighthouse CI (a11y = 100), installability e2e, Dependabot for npm. [Tasks](002-ci-verify/tasks.md).
* [003: Deploy to Azure Static Web Apps](003-deploy/spec.md) - Stage 1. Headers from one config file served locally and by Azure, CSP-clean build, preview per PR with a remote header test, production on main after green gates. Done: live at https://mango-rock-0416a080f.3.azurestaticapps.net. [Tasks](003-deploy/tasks.md).
* [004: Clickable mock-ups and the visual direction](004-mockups/spec.md) - Stage 1.5. Static HTML/Tailwind mock-ups viewed on a computer and on the phone over the home Wi-Fi; Material 3 style (ADR-0005 proposed), Liczenie (Counting) as the main screen in the M3 supporting-pane layout, a navigation bar on phones and a rail from 600 px; final M3 tokens in tokens.css, theme-color from --bg and separate any/maskable icons; outcome in [design](../design/index.md). Done. [Tasks](004-mockups/tasks.md).
* [006: Counting core](006-counting-core/spec.md) - Stage 2, part 1. Board format v1 with a validator and his board as the default, the sentence with undo, the counting state machine (every state and signal explicit, time injected), signal input with keys off by default, on-device speech; no UI (spec 007). Done. [Tasks](006-counting-core/tasks.md).
* [007: Screens](007-screens/spec.md) - Stage 2, part 2. Angular Material on our tokens (spike first), the shell with our navigation bar and rail, Liczenie (Counting) with signal buttons and touch through the machine, settings, boards with import, the sentence history, all on the device. Done. [Tasks](007-screens/tasks.md).
* [008: Liczenie (Counting) for a listener](008-listener/spec.md) - Stage 2 follow-up. He builds words by ear, not by reading: head shortcuts said aloud (a nod without a number = space, which says the word; a shake = undo, said), after reading aloud Blinker asks "Nowe zdanie?" (New sentence?), the sentence big and the prompts small; a "Spacja" (Space) button for taps. Done. [Tasks](008-listener/tasks.md).
* [009: The letter by ear](009-letter-by-ear/spec.md) - Stage 2 follow-up. Only the chosen letter is said; a pause takes the column and the letter, blinking again is the next letter, a shake undoes it; the nod per number and the spoken prompts are switches, off by default. Done. [Tasks](009-letter-by-ear/tasks.md).
* [010: Face-tracking spike](010-face-spike/spec.md) - Stage 3. MediaPipe Face Landmarker in a module worker (self-hosted, metrics to Google blocked) and the Diagnostyka (Diagnostics) screen for measurements on his devices; ADR-0006 proposed. Done (measurements pending). [Tasks](010-face-spike/tasks.md).
* [011: His signals from the camera](011-signals/spec.md) - Stage 4. A pure detector for long blink, look at his hand, nod, shake and head right with a calibrated profile on the device; signals in Diagnostyka (Diagnostics); the Kalibracja (Calibration) wizard in the navigation. Done (thresholds to check with him). [Tasks](011-signals/tasks.md).
* [012: The camera drives Liczenie (Counting)](012-camera-counting/spec.md) - Stage 5. With the camera switch on, his face signals go to the counting machine like the buttons; a sound for each signal; the face-in-view status; the rear camera for a caregiver. Done (to try with him). [Tasks](012-camera-counting/tasks.md).
* [013: Delete all data from this device](013-delete-all-data/spec.md) - Security pass, F1. One button in Ustawienia (Settings) removes everything Blinker keeps on the device after a confirmation; every stored key under one prefix. Done (e2e written in the pass of 2026-11-20). [Tasks](013-delete-all-data/tasks.md).
* [014: The camera stops in the background and can be turned off by hand](014-camera-in-background/spec.md) - Security pass, F2. The camera is released when the page is hidden and starts again by itself on return; the detector, Kalibracja (Calibration) and Diagnostyka (Diagnostics) begin cleanly; a "Kamera" (Camera) toggle in Liczenie (Counting) and a camera button in Kalibracja (Calibration) and Diagnostyka (Diagnostics). Done (e2e written in the pass of 2026-11-20; the checks on his phone wait). [Tasks](014-camera-in-background/tasks.md).
* [015: The look at his hand is a quick turn of the head](015-hand-by-head/spec.md) - Stage 5 follow-up. His hand (+5) is a head turn to his left and straight back, detected like a nod (no hold), told apart from a nod, a shake and head right; Kalibracja (Calibration) learns its head angle in one hand step; Diagnostyka (Diagnostics) copies the whole profile and signed angles. Left hand only for now. Done (to try with him; e2e written in the pass of 2026-11-20). [Tasks](015-hand-by-head/tasks.md).
* [016: Word prediction for Polish](016-word-prediction/spec.md) - Ideas. Up to 3 guesses for the word he is spelling, in a row above the board (a tap takes one) and, as a second option, asked aloud after a pause (a nod takes it, a blink goes on, a shake refuses it, one shake undoes it). A self-hosted list of 20,000 Polish words (ADR-0007) and his own words on the device; a plain letter also stands for its Polish letter. Off by default. Approved 2026-11-20; T1 and T2 done (the word list and the pure predictor, not wired into the app); paused on 2026-11-22: T3-T6 wait for the author's decision after the first session with him. [Tasks](016-word-prediction/tasks.md).
* [017: A backup of his data in a file](017-backup/spec.md) - Before the first session. Ustawienia (Settings) saves everything Blinker keeps to one .json file (the sentence history only when asked, off by default) and restores such a file after a confirmation, all or nothing, validated like an imported board; a restore replaces the data, and keeps the history when the file has none. Approved 2026-11-25; done. [Tasks](017-backup/tasks.md).
* [005: Model routing and a faster pre-push](005-model-routing/spec.md) - Tooling. A model per loop step plus a difficulty label per task; the pre-push hook runs only the fast gates. [Tasks](005-model-routing/tasks.md).
