# Blinker

Blinker helps a person who communicates by blinking: a phone or tablet camera follows the face,
the app scans a letter board (column, then row), recognizes deliberate signals (long blink,
head and eye movements) and turns them into sentences that are shown and read aloud.

- Runs in the browser as an installable PWA; camera frames never leave the device.
- No accounts, no backend, no analytics.
- UI in English and Polish.

> Blinker is not a medical device. Keep the paper board as a fallback.
